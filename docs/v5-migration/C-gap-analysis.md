## Executive Summary

Most of this corpus is not in conflict with Anthropic's v5 guidance. The 44 files are structured with markdown headings, bold labels, tables, and fenced code blocks — which is precisely what Anthropic prescribes for the one artifact class it prescribes structure for. Twelve of the nineteen workflow invariants are enforced by a hook or CLI rather than by prose, which is exactly the mechanism Anthropic names as the only reliable one. Personas are one sentence. Sub-agent context is front-loaded by platform injection. Roughly 2,661 lines of coordinator reference material are already kept out of the always-loaded budget. These are not accidents to be preserved by luck; they are the shape Anthropic recommends, and § Validated Practices enumerates them so a rewrite cannot discard them.

The divergences split three ways.

**Rewording only, no restructuring.** Two gaps are pure wording: unpaired negative examples where Anthropic states three separate times that positive framing steers style and format better (G13), and rule copies whose scope is stated once and therefore will not be generalized under v5 literalism (G11).

**Restructuring, with real arithmetic behind it.** The always-injected shared layer is 917 lines against Anthropic's 200-line-per-file target — 2.65x and 1.94x over — and roughly 82% of the project-root file plus roughly 30% of the global file is narrow, task-specific reference material paid for in every session and every sub-agent spawn (G1, G2). Seven skill files exceed the 500-line Agent Skills cap, the largest at 1,332 lines (G5). Substantial project-and-convention content sits in output styles, which Anthropic's only explicit output-style misuse warning says belongs in CLAUDE.md (G14).

**Outright defects.** Nine of Document B's twenty-one catalogued tensions are correctness failures, not style observations: instructions to query an MCP server no sub-agent can reach, a return contract that exists in zero of seventeen agent definitions while three competing formats do, an exclusion set present in one coordinator prompt and missing from the other, a reference doc teaching a card format the CLI rejects, and a 341-line file documenting two tools the repository no longer ships.

**Two things the evidence refuses to support, and they matter as much as the gaps.** Anthropic is silent on emphasis technique across all seven research passes — so no case for removing ALL-CAPS, emoji sirens, or modal escalation may cite it, and the 🚨 siren's reactive-scar-tissue rationale is independently refuted by its non-reactive origin commit. And there is no official length guidance of any kind for output styles or agent definitions, in either direction, so the two 3,000-line output styles cannot be indicted on length by citing Anthropic. Both sets of proposals live in § Gaps Without Official Authority.

**What the amendment changed in this summary's own terms.** The register is now **eighteen gaps, not nineteen** — G17 was refuted and relocated to NA7, because Anthropic's "large or complex" is an adjective and not a threshold. **G11 is high, not blocking.** **G12 is narrowed to its hookable subset.** **VP3 protects the reason, not the provenance.** **D32 is now in evidence *against* relocating `kanban-cli/SKILL.md:31-254`, and both G5 and G6 carry it** — the rarest kind of finding this effort produced, because it is Anthropic authority for leaving something alone. Two directives the original document never mentioned turned out to be **additional validated practices** (D4, D25 → VP10, VP11), so the validated-to-gap ratio is eleven-to-eighteen rather than nine-to-nineteen. **The measurement pass closed three coverage gaps**: the session model is pinned in the launcher shellapps (**Opus 5 guidance governs both coordinator tiers; Sonnet 5 guidance governs every sub-agent**), the review harness carries **no** severity-gating language (a clean negative, D9-compliant), and D42 is measured (**16 of 17 descriptions are trigger-oriented; `swe-security` is the lone violation; three routing-collision pairs are unhandled**). **Two new coverage gaps were opened rather than resolved:** the `--effort xhigh` pin on both coordinator tiers, and D20's do-not-reason check against the corpus's concision directives.

**And one thing the amendment deliberately did not change: this document's bias.** The adversarial verification measured it by what it could find that the document missed and reported that the bias *"runs mildly against change, not toward it"* — and that the document's actual failure mode was *"thoroughness producing a register with one entry too many (G17), not advocacy producing an inflated one."* **Nothing in the amendment makes this document read as more decisive than the evidence supports.** § Non-Gaps and § Coverage Gaps remain deliberately unflattering to their own inputs, which is the property the verification singled out as load-bearing.

**The single highest-leverage change is the 917-line always-injected layer.** It is the only content all 44 contexts pay for, the only artifact class with a citable Anthropic number attached to *adherence* rather than cost alone, and it is roughly 2.3x over that number in aggregate. Every other length finding in this document is a cost argument; this one is a compliance argument.

### 🚨 HARD PREREQUISITE ON DOCUMENT D — compute the Stage 1 line budget before committing to the 200-line target

**This is the single largest behavioral risk in the plan, and this document created it by not doing the arithmetic.** § Prioritization Stage 1 states that G1 requires roughly **517 lines** to leave the two always-injected files. § Workflow Invariant Risk Assessment states that **five prompt-only invariants live wholly or partly inside those same 917 lines** — WI-9's draft-first and banned-phrasing halves (`CLAUDE.md:395-405`, `:407-440`), WI-12's enumerated prohibited-target categories (`:37-51`), WI-16's shell and search rules (`:343-355`, `:356-363`, `:372-385`), WI-17 (`:7`, `:15`, `:144`, `:387-391`), and WI-18's destructive-operation set (`:26-35`, `:53-62`). § Prioritization Stage 1 then states that **none of them may be shortened**. This document never checks whether 517 lines can be found with those sections excluded.

**The requirement, stated so it cannot be skimmed past.** Document D **must show the line budget closes after excluding the off-limits invariant sections** — an explicit subtraction: total always-injected lines, minus the enumerated invariant sections that may not be touched, minus any section a hard sequencing constraint parks elsewhere, against the 517-line reduction target. If that arithmetic does not close, then **Stage 1's target is unreachable and something protected will be cut to hit the number** — and the thing cut will be protected only by the fact that it is written down, so nothing will fail when it goes. The adversarial verification states the consequence in its own words: *"or the target will be met by cutting something that is only protected by the fact that it is written down"* (`.scratchpad/C-verification.md`, § Overall Assessment).

**What this prerequisite does not license.** It is not an argument that the 200-line target should be abandoned, and it is not permission to declare the target unreachable without doing the subtraction. It is a requirement that the subtraction be *shown*. If the budget does not close, the correct outcomes are to relocate more narrow content (G2), to accept a documented overshoot with the reason stated, or to escalate to the owner — never to shorten an invariant section to make a number work.

### G1 — The two always-injected CLAUDE.md files are 2.65x and 1.94x over Anthropic's stated per-file target

**What Anthropic says.** Document A, D33 and § CLAUDE.md And Memory Files: "**Size**: target under 200 lines per CLAUDE.md file. Longer files consume more context and **reduce adherence**." Restated on the same page: "Files over 200 lines consume more context and may reduce adherence," and "CLAUDE.md files are loaded in full regardless of length, though shorter files produce better adherence." Document A's § Emphasis And Over-Steering finding 8 records that this is the **only** statement in the entire corpus tying a numeric length figure to adherence rather than to cost, and that it is stated four times on one page.

**What we currently do.** `modules/claude/global/CLAUDE.md` is 530 lines and the project-root `CLAUDE.md` is 387 — Document B § Configuration Inventory, Tier 1, "**Tier total: 917 lines across 2 files**", with Tier-1 membership confirmed empirically rather than assumed (a background sub-agent received both files in its leading `claudeMd` block before any task instruction). Section anchors across the global file at `CLAUDE.md:26-35`, `:37-51`, `:53-62`, `:343-355`, `:372-385`, `:395-405`, `:407-440`, `:442-449`, `:457-465`, `:506-525` and across the project file at `CLAUDE.md:9`, `:13`, `:32`, `:72`, `:106`, `:160`, `:167`, `:175`, `:228`, `:310`, `:331`, `:351`, `:355`, `:378`.

**Nature of the divergence.** **Actively works against.** Every other length finding in this document costs context without causing harm. This one has an Anthropic-stated adherence consequence attached, on the two files that reach every context. A rule the model is less likely to follow is worse than a rule that merely costs tokens.

**Blast radius.** 2 files, 917 lines, paid unconditionally in every session and every sub-agent spawn. Target compliance would mean roughly 400 lines total, so roughly 517 lines must relocate or be deleted.

**Severity: blocking** for Stage 1 — every other Stage-1 decision is downstream of where those 517 lines go.

**Behavioral risk of closing it.** High and concrete. The always-injected layer is where the hard prohibitions live — `CLAUDE.md:26-35` (outright prohibitions including never-skip-hooks), `CLAUDE.md:53-62` (the ask-first destructive set). Document B classifies WI-17 and WI-18 as **prompt-only** with no mechanical backstop, so shortening by deletion in those sections removes a guarantee outright rather than reformatting it. The safe reduction is relocation of narrow content (G2), not compression of prohibitions.

### G2 — Most of the always-injected layer is content Anthropic's own boundary test says belongs elsewhere

**What Anthropic says.** Document A, D36: "Keep it to facts Claude should hold in every session: build commands, conventions, project layout, 'always do X' rules. If an entry is a multi-step procedure or only matters for one part of the codebase, move it to a skill or a path-scoped rule instead." **What we currently do.** Document B § Configuration Inventory classifies the project-root file's H2 sections by audience breadth and finds roughly **317 of 387 lines (~82%)** narrow and task-specific: macOS Trash CLI (`CLAUDE.md:13-30`), Team Member Terminology (`:72-104`), Quick Commands (`:106-158`), Configuration Structure (`:167-173`), Development Workflows (`:175-227`), Scripting Principles (`:228-309`), File Management (`:310-329`), Claude Code Integration (`:331-349`), Reference Documentation (`:355-376`), External References (`:378-387`). The global file's comparable share is roughly 160 of 530 lines (~30%). Several of these are multi-step procedures by Anthropic's own test — the four-step "Add new package" recipe at `CLAUDE.md:183-190` and the `git add` → `hms` → `commit` → `push` order at `:177-181`.

**AMENDED — the ~82% and ~30% figures are upper bounds on relocatable content, not measurements.** Per `.scratchpad/C-verification.md` § Overstated Gaps: *"`~82%` narrow in the project-root file and `~30%` in the global file are audience-breadth classifications, not counts."* They are **judgments about audience breadth**, and the classification is contestable in at least one named case the verification checked directly: § Scripting Principles (`CLAUDE.md:228`, 82 lines, this document's single largest narrow block) contains the paragraph *"This applies to code review too. Do not flag missing dependency handling… as a deficiency when the dependency is Nix-guaranteed"* — **a review-time rule, not a shellapp-authoring rule**, and review happens in most sessions. So part of the largest "narrow" block is broad-audience content misclassified as narrow.

This document applies exactly this upper-bound discipline to Document B's 23.1% accretion signal (see the note on the accretion hypothesis above, and § Prioritization Stage 1) and dropped it here. The correct reading, matching the discipline applied elsewhere: **~82% and ~30% are ceilings on how much could be relocated, and the true relocatable figure is lower by an unmeasured amount.** Document D must not treat 477 lines as a budget it can spend; it must re-derive the figure section by section, applying `/doctor`'s explicit *keep pitfalls* policy (quoted above) as the test rather than audience breadth alone. This does not weaken G2 — the boundary is real, the practice is real, and the direction of the finding is unchanged.

**Nature of the divergence.** **Merely inefficient**, and Document B is emphatic that this must not be conflated with accretion: "text can be perfectly deliberate in origin and still be irrelevant to most of the contexts it is injected into." No behavioral harm is documented for irrelevant-but-correct context. It becomes an adherence problem only through G1's length channel.

**Blast radius.** ~317 lines of 387 in the project-root file plus ~160 of 530 in the global file — roughly 477 of 917 always-injected lines. **Read as an upper bound per the amendment above, not as a relocation budget.**

**Severity: high.**

**Behavioral risk of closing it.** Moderate. The risk is misclassification: `CLAUDE.md:13-30` (macOS Trash) reads as narrow but encodes a pitfall that already caused real damage, and `/doctor`'s policy explicitly *keeps* pitfalls. Relocating a pitfall to an on-demand surface means it is absent exactly when a session stumbles into it unprepared. Note also the mechanism constraint from Document A D37 — see § Non-Gaps: `@path` imports cannot achieve this relocation.
